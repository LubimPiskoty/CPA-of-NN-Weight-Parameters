\chapter{Teamwork, Diversity and Inclusion}
\label{chap:teamwork}
% FIIT: communication, distribution of roles, reaching consensus; ~0.5 page.

The thesis is an individual work.
The author designed the firmware, the capture setup and the analysis, and wrote the text.
The supervisor, Xiaolu Hou, set the research direction and gave feedback on the approach and on the results.

Communication with the supervisor was ad hoc, whenever a question or a result needed discussion.
The supervisor gave feedback on the work and guided its direction, for example the choice of the threat model and the evaluation, and the decisions were agreed on together.
The work plan in Appendix~B records how the tasks were divided over time.

The repository is public and documented, so other students can join or continue the work without a personal introduction.
The text and the documentation use plain language and avoid unexplained abbreviations, to be readable for people with different backgrounds.
