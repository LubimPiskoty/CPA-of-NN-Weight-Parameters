\chapter{Risk Assessment}
\label{chap:risks}
% FIIT: SWOT analysis and mitigation strategies; ~1 page.

\paragraph{Strengths.}
Exact \texttt{float32} recovery ($100\,\%$ success from $263$ attack traces),
inexpensive hardware, and public code and data.

\paragraph{Weaknesses.}
Only one weight is attacked, on one unprotected target using software
floating-point, and profiling and attack share a board.

\paragraph{Opportunities.}
Extension to more weights and devices, and evaluation of countermeasures.

\paragraph{Threats.}
Dependence on one board and on changing ChipWhisperer software, and misuse
against deployed models.

\paragraph{Mitigation.}
Results are claimed only for the evaluated setting, and template transfer to a
second board is left as future work. Stored traces and pinned library versions keep the
analysis runnable without the board (Chapter~\ref{chap:reproducibility}). The
attack targets only our own test network, to help designers protect devices.
