\chapter{Employability}
\label{chap:employability}
% FIIT: skills gained and competitiveness on the job market; ~0.5 page.

The work gave me practical experience in embedded security and signal processing.
I learned to program and flash a microcontroller in C, to control a ChipWhisperer capture setup, and to run a measurement campaign with a reproducible configuration.
On the analysis side, I applied statistical methods (Gaussian templates, point-of-interest selection, success-rate estimation) in Python with NumPy and SciPy.

I also gained general skills: reading and comparing research papers, designing and documenting an experiment, presenting results in figures, and writing a technical text in \LaTeX{}.
Working with Git and a pinned environment is a standard practice in industry.

These skills are relevant for roles in hardware and embedded security, security evaluation of devices, and machine-learning engineering on edge devices.
Knowledge of how implementation details leak information is also useful in software development in general.
